## What's new in 1.9.0

- **Find any setting.** Type part of a setting's name in the command palette (e.g. "break remin") and Enter jumps straight to it, with its card highlighted.
- **Pinned tools.** The Dashboard has a row of your favourite pages. Right-click any page in the sidebar to pin or unpin it.
- **Window Layouts.** Save where your windows are as a named layout (Gaming, Study, Streaming…) and put them all back in one click or from the palette, optionally opening apps that aren't running.
- **Now playing.** A mini player in the sidebar shows the song from Spotify, Apple Music, foobar2000, MusicBee, TIDAL, or YouTube and YouTube Music in your browser, with previous, play/pause and next. Can be turned off in Settings.
- **Per-game resolution.** Give a game profile its own resolution and refresh rate (say 1080p at 360 Hz). Game Mode switches to it while you play and back afterwards, even if the app closed unexpectedly.
- **Power timer.** On the Tools page: shut down, restart or sleep after a set time, or once your browser and Steam downloads have finished. A one-minute countdown always lets you cancel.

### Changes
- Backups can now include your personal data (play history, screen time, input stats, notes, benchmarks and layouts). Export asks first, so a setup shared with friends stays settings-only.
- All features keep their data in one folder next to settings.json.
