## What's new in 1.5.0

PC Optimizer now does more than tune your PC: there are two new sidebar sections, **Play** and **Everyday**.

- **Game Library.** Every installed game from Steam, Epic and GOG in one place, with Steam's cover art and playtime. Search, sort by recent or most played, and click a cover to play (Game Mode can switch on automatically).
- **Clips & Screenshots.** **Ctrl+Shift+F9** saves the last 30 seconds of gameplay (using Windows Game Bar's background recording, which you can turn on from the page). **Ctrl+Shift+F10** saves a screenshot named after the game. A gallery shows everything you've captured.
- **FPS in the overlay.** The performance overlay now shows your real frame rate and 1% lows in DirectX games (needs administrator mode).
- **Clipboard history.** **Ctrl+Alt+V** opens your last 50 copies anywhere; type to filter and press Enter to paste. It's kept in memory only, and password-manager copies are never saved. Plus quick notes that save as you type.
- **Screen Time.** See how long you spend in each app per day and across the week, only counting time you're actually at the PC. Optional break reminders. Nothing leaves your PC.
