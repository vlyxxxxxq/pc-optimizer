## What's new in 1.8.0

- **Screen recorder.** Record any part of your screen, or a whole screen, as an MP4 or a GIF. Press Ctrl+Shift+F11 (changeable) or click "Record an area" on Clips & Screenshots, drag out an area or click for the whole screen, then press the hotkey or Stop to save. MP4 uses Windows' own H.264 encoder, on your GPU when it can. GIFs last up to 60 seconds, are at most 960 px wide, and store only what changed between frames, so they stay small. The timer bar and the frame around the area never show up in the recording. Recordings save to Videos\PC Optimizer and appear in the gallery. Video only, no sound.
- **Input Stats.** Count your key presses, clicks, scrolling and cursor travel, with a keyboard heatmap, a mouse heatmap across all your screens, a 7-day chart, and per-game stats (APM and most-used keys) while Game Mode is on. Only counts are kept, never what you type, and nothing leaves your PC. It's off until you switch on "Count my input".

### Changes
- The command palette has a "Record screen" action.
- The gallery labels each item as a clip, recording, GIF or screenshot.
