## PC Optimizer is now Rig (2.0.0)

Rig is the all-in-one version of PC Optimizer: everything it did, plus everyday tools, a new look and a Home you
arrange yourself. PC Optimizer offers this update like any other; after one click it restarts as Rig, with all
your settings and history. The project now lives at github.com/vlyxxxxxq/rig (old links redirect).

### New look
- **Sidebar in five categories**: Gaming, Media, Productivity, Files and System. Fold categories you don't use,
  or collapse the sidebar to icons.
- **Light theme and accent colours**: Settings → Appearance (dark stays the default; six accents).
- **Modules**: switch off any tool in Settings → Modules. It leaves the sidebar and stops its background work.
  Pages now load the first time you open them, so the app starts lighter.

### Home
- The Dashboard became **Home**: a grid of cards you choose, move and remove (Customize). Cards for pinned tools,
  PC health, CPU, memory, system, Game Mode with your last session, now playing, screen time, input, timers, the
  power timer, free games and storage.

### New tools
- **Timers & Reminders**: countdowns, reminders at a time or after a while (they survive restarts) and a stopwatch.
  Also from the palette: "remind me in 20 min to stretch", "timer 25m focus".
- **Batch Rename**: find and replace, numbering, prefixes and suffixes, case, with a live preview and undo.
- **Similar Photos**: finds near-duplicate photos, even resized or re-saved copies, keeps the best of each and
  recycles the rest.
- **Color Picker & Ruler**: Ctrl+Shift+F7 freezes the screen with a magnifier; click to copy a colour's HEX
  (RGB and HSL too), or drag to measure in pixels.
- **Palette quick answers**: units ("5 ft in cm"), currency ("100 usd to eur", European Central Bank rates),
  emoji (":fire") and time zones ("time in tokyo").

### Moving over from PC Optimizer
- When PC Optimizer updates itself into Rig, the first start moves the program to its new folder and renames the
  Start menu and desktop shortcuts, the "Installed apps" entry, start with Windows and the maintenance task.
  Settings, games, play history, notes and everything else are copied to `%AppData%\Rig`.

### Changes
- Drop-down lists now match the theme.
- **Beta updates**: switch them on in Settings → Updates to get new versions early.
